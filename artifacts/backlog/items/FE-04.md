## What

Written in clusters — data, separation, leakage, evaluation, reproducibility,
production, framing last — with all twenty-three must-never rules quoted verbatim at their mapped
homes, and the evaluation philosophy placed as requirements in Standards 19 and 20 rather than as
a twenty-sixth standard.

## Why

The brief presents the evaluation philosophy as cross-cutting. Making it a standard would
have broken the 1:1 item-number bijection that `Source: item N` and `implementedBy` bind to.

## Notes

Framing (Standard 1) was written last because it references everything else.
