## What

A positive verdict now requires that at least one applicable rule was established. Where
nothing was established the status is `NOT_EVALUATED`, which already exits 2.

## Why

`compliance.mjs` opened by claiming there is no default-pass path. That held for
individual rules and did not hold for the verdict assembled from them: the status ladder ended in
a bare `else`, so an evaluation in which every applicable rule was skipped arrived at
`COMPLIANT` — nothing failed, therefore everything passed.
