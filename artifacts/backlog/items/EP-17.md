## What

The files that state which version this framework is, and which version a reviewed artifact
corresponds to, agree with each other and with the catalog they describe.

Its subject is **provenance, not publication**. Cutting a tag, pushing, and choosing what a
future release carries are outside it. The outcome is discharged when a reader of `VERSION`,
`package.json`, `CHANGELOG.md` and `artifacts/catalog-baseline.json` reaches the same answer
about what version the current catalog is.

## Why

No existing epic owned this. [[EP-15]] owns the corpus and the catalog agreeing about what each
rule *is*, and that outcome is discharged; [[EP-12]] owns normative additions awaiting a MAJOR
vehicle. Neither covers a version-bearing field disagreeing with the artifact it annotates, which
is how `rules/evaluation.json` came to assert `introducedIn: "2.0.0"` for eight days while
`VERSION` read `1.6.0` and nothing compared the two.

Filing it as an epic rather than hanging the work off a closed one keeps [[EP-15]]'s discharged
outcome true. Its own note records that a closed epic's outcome is not reopened by later work
that merely touches the same file.

## Outcome

**The four version-bearing records agree.** `VERSION` and `package.json` read `2.0.0`,
`CHANGELOG.md` carries a measured 2.0.0 entry, and `artifacts/catalog-baseline.json` names
`2.0.0` as the version its locked rules correspond to. `rules/evaluation.json`'s
`introducedIn: "2.0.0"` now resolves to a version that exists.

Closed on its stated outcome, not on the release event: no tag exists and none is authorised,
which is why the epic was scoped to the records rather than to publication.

## Notes

**Most of this epic's work landed before the epic existed.** Commit `082ecba` on 2026-09-04 cut
2.0.0 — `VERSION` and `package.json` to `2.0.0`, a measured CHANGELOG entry, and the retirement
of the baseline `$comment` claim *"No release carries this yet"* that the release falsified. It
is recorded here by its sha rather than itemised retrospectively; the precedent for saying so
plainly rather than reconstructing tracking after the fact is `932557b`.

**Publication is deliberately not in scope.** No tag exists for 2.0.0 and none is authorised.
An epic scoped to the release *event* would sit open on an action nobody may take; this one is
scoped to the records, so it can honestly close when they agree.
