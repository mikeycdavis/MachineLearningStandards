## What

Bootstrap, evidence discovery, the verdict, per-rule explanation, and decision
freshness — with the exit contract 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 uniform across all of them.

## Why

Exit 3 exists so that a refusal on invariant grounds is machine-visible rather than
indistinguishable from a rule failing.

## Notes

`plan` was rejected as a separate command because `init --dry-run` is the plan: both
derive from one `plan()`, so dry-run cannot disagree with apply. `check` folded into `evaluate`.
