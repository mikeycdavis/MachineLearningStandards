## What

A check that compares each catalog entry's `kind` against the normative strength of the standard
section it names, and reports disagreement — closing the one provenance link in this repository
that has no guard.

Three links carry a claim from one representation to another. Two are already mechanical:

| Link | Guard |
| --- | --- |
| spec → prose | `scripts/fidelity.mjs` — every "reproduced verbatim" claim is checked character-for-character |
| catalog → baseline | `scripts/integrity.mjs` — `{kind, level, severity, verification, assurance, nonExemptible}` locked against `artifacts/catalog-baseline.json` |
| **prose → catalog** | **nothing** |

`integrity.mjs` never opens `standards/`; `fidelity.mjs` never opens `rules/`. Nothing in the
repository compares a standard's modal verb to its entry's kind, which is why the five
disagreements in [[FE-32]] stood from `1.0.0` to `2026-08-26` without any run ever failing.

## Why

This is a **framework gap, not an assurance boundary**, and the distinction is the one
[[FE-26]] draws. An assurance boundary is where a mechanism genuinely cannot establish the thing —
the ten-of-forty-six automation figure is that, and correctly gets no item. Here a mechanism can
establish it: the check was performed by hand while writing
[ADR 0011](../../adr/0011-the-catalog-states-a-rules-level.md), over the whole corpus, and produced
a definite result. Something performed by hand and then not kept is a gap awaiting work.

It also matters that `1.0.0` is what would have caught this. A guard built now catches the *next*
one, which is the only kind of guard worth building.

## The corpus shape this must confront

Measured at `87b31e8`:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Prose `### R` / `### P` sections | 135 |
| Catalog entries | 46 — 18 requirement, 21 prohibition, 7 recommendation |
| Prose sections with **no** entry | 89 |

**A one-to-one checker is wrong and must not be built.** The catalog has never been a projection of
the prose. `frameworkCoverage` exists precisely to report the shortfall beside the verdict, and a
standard may state an obligation the framework does not carry as a rule — most of them do. The 89
are out of scope **by construction**, not by exemption: the check runs entry → section, never
section → entry, and an unrepresented section is not an input to it.

**A naive modal-verb comparison is also wrong**, and this was measured rather than supposed. Run
across all 46 entries, a first-bolded-modal test flags every one of the 21 prohibition entries: a
`## Prohibitions` section quotes its must-never bullet verbatim and carries no bolded `MUST NOT` at
all. Twenty-one false positives out of forty-six is not a check, it is noise that would be switched
off. The comparison is only meaningful for the 25 requirement and recommendation entries, whose
sections follow the corpus convention of a leading bolded normative sentence. A prohibition entry's
agreement with its section is established a different way — the section quotes the source — and
whether that is worth checking at all is part of this item's design, not an assumption in it.

Two further hazards, both hit while measuring:

- **Scoping to the whole section over-reports.** A section body quotes the source, contrasts a
  failing case, and discusses neighbours; scanning all of it found `MUST` in sections whose own
  normative sentence says `SHOULD`. The comparison must bind to the document's stated convention —
  the first bolded normative sentence — and nothing else.
- **The mapping must be exact.** Every one of the 46 entries carries both `standard` and
  `requirement`, so the target section is named rather than inferred. An entry whose section does
  not resolve is a finding in its own right, never a silent skip.

## Completion condition

1. A design record naming the check's scope, its inputs, and — following
   [`design/ml-audit-detectors.md`](../../../design/ml-audit-detectors.md) and the discipline
   [[FE-09]] set — **what it deliberately does not check**, with the 89 unrepresented sections and
   the prohibition convention both stated as scope decisions rather than omissions.
2. The check implemented, zero-dependency, exiting on the repository's existing contract.
3. **Paired fixtures per branch**: one that must provoke the finding and one that must not. False
   positives are the historically shipped bug class here, and this check has 21 candidate ones.
4. A decision on whether it joins `scripts/ci-pipeline.mjs` as a gate — see below.

## Notes

**It cannot become a CI gate before [[FE-32]] resolves.** Run today it correctly reports five
findings, so adding it to the pipeline would turn `main` red on a defect that is known, recorded,
and deliberately unrepaired. That is a sequencing fact, not a blocker on building it: a check that
truthfully reports five real defects is a working check, and building it before the repair is what
proves the repair. Whether it gates is the fourth completion item precisely so the answer is
recorded rather than assumed.

**`NOT_STARTED` means not scheduled, not un-actionable.** Everything it needs is present.

**It does not extend `EVALUATED_RULES`.** This checks *this repository's own* corpus against
*this repository's own* catalog, the way `inventory` and `fidelity` do. It says nothing about a
target project and adds no detector, so it is unrelated to the untracked detector-language
coverage question, which stays out of the backlog.
