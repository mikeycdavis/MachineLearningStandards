## What

The entity model, the conclusion vocabulary, the concept investigation, and the five
decision records — written before any implementation.

## Why

The ordering was not negotiable: standards cite specification items, catalog entries
bind to standard numbers and heading anchors, and the evaluated set binds to rule ids. Deciding
that after building it would have meant retrofitting prose around what a regex happens to match.
