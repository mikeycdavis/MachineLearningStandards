comes from `evaluate`, not `scan`; the target is given as `--dir=<path>`; the status vocabulary is
the five values this pack emits.

## Why

the documented invocation directly, and requires the two results to be identical. A declaration
that drifts from the CLI it describes fails this pack's own suite, which is what makes it evidence
rather than a comment.
