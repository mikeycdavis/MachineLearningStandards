## What

The catalog, the invariants, the policy schema, five commands, ten detectors, and the
self-evaluation — everything needed to reach a verdict and to say what the verdict does not cover.

## Why

An enforcer that overstates its reach is worse than none. Coverage travels beside the
verdict, never inside it.
