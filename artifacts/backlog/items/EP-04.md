## What

`rules/*.json`, `rules/invariants.json`, `schemas/project-policy.schema.json`, and the
verdict engine in `scripts/compliance.mjs`.

## Why

The three-way separation is the core invariant: the catalog defines identity and
metadata, policy defines applicability, the evaluator produces evidence, and none may redefine
the others.
