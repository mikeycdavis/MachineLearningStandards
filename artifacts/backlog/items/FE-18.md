## What

All ten machine-examined rules classified against one question: when this detector emits
nothing, what does that mean? Seven sound, three not.

## Why

The audit ran before the remediation, so that fixing the observed cases could not conceal
a general problem.

## Notes

One of the three was found by the audit rather than by any target. That is the argument
for auditing the whole set instead of the cases that happened to surface.
