## What

`design/architecture.md` with the full concept-investigation table — every candidate
concept marked adopted, adapted or rejected with reasoning — plus ADRs 0001 through 0005.

## Why

The rejected column is the load-bearing one. Recording that evaluative decision rules
were rejected, and why, is what stops a later maintainer reintroducing them as an obvious
improvement.

## Notes

Two decisions here shaped everything after: prohibitions and invariants are first-class
kinds rather than flags on requirements (ADR 0003), and automation is bounded by honesty with
eight checks deliberately not built (ADR 0004).
