## What

Canonical `category.kebab-case-name` ids where the category always equals the id prefix
and the filename, with no aliases ever; then the catalog entries themselves — requirements,
prohibitions and recommendations, each carrying verification, assurance, exemptibility and
lifecycle fields.

## Why

Aliases are the mechanism by which a rule gets referred to two ways and then diverges.
The schema's `ruleId` pattern rejected other spellings from the first commit.

## Notes

Every one of the brief's must-never rules is carried by exactly one `kind: prohibition`
entry. Seven are `nonExemptible`, on one criterion: an exception would be written permission to
deceive.
