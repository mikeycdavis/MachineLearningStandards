## What

Twenty-five standards derived from the source brief through a numbered specification,
with the derivation itself checked by two guards rather than asserted.

## Why

A standard that paraphrases its source no longer says what its author wrote, and a
corpus that drops a topic cannot notice. Provenance had to be mechanical or it would be a claim
like any other.
