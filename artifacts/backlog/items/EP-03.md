## What

`standards/NN-*.md`, one per specification item, each with Scope, Requirements,
Prohibitions, Evidence and verification, Additions, and Implementation.

## Why

The catalog formalises an already-reviewed standard. Had the catalog come first, the
rule set would have been whatever the detectors could find — a small fraction of what matters in
this domain.
