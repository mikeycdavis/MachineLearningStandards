## What

Three fixes: a rule whose detector is gated behind a trigger no longer reports `passed`
when the trigger never fired; a blocked run suppresses the score; and `explain` resolves to a real
file and a verified heading anchor instead of emitting `standards/15-*.md`.

## Why

The first is the general form of the bug the scaffolding case was one instance of — a
project with no data was being credited with documenting its data. A number computed with a ruler
the framework has just called untrustworthy should not be published.
