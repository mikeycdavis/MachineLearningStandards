## What

A standalone standards system for machine-learning work: normative documents, a
machine-readable catalog, and tooling that reaches a verdict on a repository without
overstating what it checked.

## Why

Machine-learning failure modes — leakage, a spent test set, a metric chosen after the
result — are not caught by general engineering standards, and they are not caught by prose
nobody can evaluate. The value is a standard that is both normative and mechanically checkable,
where the mechanical part is honest about its own reach.
