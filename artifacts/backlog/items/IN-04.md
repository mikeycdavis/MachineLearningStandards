## What

The acceptance lock, the review that motivated it, and the first release to change the
normative corpus — plus the supported normative work that a MINOR release cannot carry.

## Why

A standards-integrity guard that locks a rule's classification but not its satisfaction
semantics cannot establish that the rule was not weakened.

## Notes

Open because EP-12 holds three supported requirement additions that need a MAJOR
release, and one finding that must re-enter disposition under the correct class.
