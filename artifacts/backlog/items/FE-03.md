## What

`artifacts/standards-source-inventory.json`, human-reviewed and never regenerated, plus
`scripts/inventory.mjs` and `scripts/fidelity.mjs`.

## Why

A lock that a run can rewrite is not a lock. Extraction is compared against the committed
inventory, so a change to the parser cannot silently redefine how many standards exist.

## Notes

Both guards were made green over zero standards. That is the only moment they are
cheap: adding them afterwards means auditing every document that already exists.
