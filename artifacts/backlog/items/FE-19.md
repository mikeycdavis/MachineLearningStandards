## What

Three corrections. Identical evidence now receives an identical disposition before and
after `git add`. Evidence must establish the subject of its rule — versioning a model is not
versioning the data. And silence is probative only where the detector had a subject to read.

## Why

Scope had been `git ls-files --cached`, so everything `init` writes was invisible until
staged: the documented workflow told operators the documents they had just written did not exist.

## Notes

The third fix is asserted from both directions. A detector whose idiom is present and
clean still passes — a check that converted every pass into a shrug would be worthless.
