## What

A published invocation contract, proof that the contract matches the CLI across a
checkout boundary, and the verdict defect that consumption exposed.

## Why

An external orchestrator has to know which command is authoritative and which statuses
pass. Guessing produces a consumer that reinterprets the pack, which is the thing this pack must
not permit.
