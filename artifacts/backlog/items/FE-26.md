## What

Whether the twenty-five standards cover the domain is not established, and nothing here
claims it is.

## Why

The six gaps found in the normative review came from one non-independent review and are
not a denominator. Adding N16 — or later N3, N5 and N8 — would not make the corpus complete.

## Notes

Recorded as an open item so that the absence of a completeness claim stays visible.
It is deliberately **not** paired with a proposed mechanism: an independent review is the only
thing that would move it, and inventing a metric here would manufacture the denominator the
changelog says does not exist.

Distinct from the ten-of-forty-five automation figure, which is an assurance boundary and not a
gap awaiting work. That one gets no backlog item, by design.
