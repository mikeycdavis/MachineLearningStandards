## What

Write down the convention `scripts/levels.mjs` reads standards by, and close the one shape in which
departing from it produces a false pass instead of a finding.

The convention, as the gate currently applies it: **in a requirement or recommendation section, the
rule's normative sentence is the first bold span that is not inside a fenced block or a
blockquote, and it carries the modal that states the rule's level.**

## Why

Two facts, both measured rather than assumed.

**No repository document states it.** `design/architecture.md` maps each kind to `### R<n>`
sections and records that every catalog entry carries a `requirement` anchor naming its heading;
`artifacts/project-plan-breakdown/02-standards.md` names the section list a standard must contain.
Neither says the normative sentence is bolded, that it comes first, or that its modal is what a
gate reads. An author adding a rule to an existing standard, or writing a twenty-sixth, has nothing
to follow but the example of the files already there.

**Departing from it can produce a false pass, not a hard failure.** The four shapes were run
against the real checker:

| Section shape | Entry kind | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bold lead carrying `MUST`, real sentence says `SHOULD` | requirement | **passes — false agreement** |
| Bold lead carrying `SHOULD`, real sentence says `MUST` | recommendation | **passes — false agreement** |
| Bold lead with no modal, real sentence beneath | recommendation | finding — `no-modal` |
| Normative sentence not bolded at all | requirement | finding — `no-normative-sentence` |

The last two fail safely, which is why the gate was accepted as sound at the time. The first two do
not: the gate resolves *a* sentence, reads its modal, and reports agreement for a rule whose prose
and catalog genuinely disagree. That is the precise defect [[FE-33]] exists to catch, passing under
the check built to catch it.

The repository's stated posture is against exactly this. `invariant.no-silent-pass` governs the
evaluator rather than the gates, so this is not a formal violation of it — but its reasoning is the
same one: *"Absence of a finding is not evidence of compliance."* A gate that reports success from
a misreading is worse than no gate, because a reader now believes something was checked.

## Completion condition

1. **The convention is stated where an author will find it** — with the standards' document shape,
   not only in the checker's header. It must say what the normative sentence is, that it is the
   first bold span, that quoted and fenced text is excluded, and that its modal is load-bearing
   because a gate reads it.
2. **A section that departs from it is a finding rather than a misreading.** The narrow form: a
   section containing more than one modal-bearing bold span is reported — `ambiguous-normative-sentence`
   or similar — instead of the first one silently winning. That turns both false-pass rows above
   into truthful failures.
3. **Paired tests**, per the discipline [[FE-09]] set and [[FE-33]] followed: the two false-pass
   shapes must produce findings, and the twenty-six real entries must stay green. A mutation must
   show the new assertion bites.
4. **[ADR 0013](../../adr/0013-the-prose-to-catalog-link-gets-a-guard.md)'s Consequences updated**,
   where it presently records the convention as load-bearing and undocumented. That paragraph is
   the finding; it should become the record of the resolution.

## Notes

**Contract-shaped, not a detector rewrite.** Nothing about what the checker examines, which
direction it walks, its scope, or its exclusions changes. It gains one finding reason for a shape
it currently resolves wrongly. The distinction matters because a rewrite would reopen decisions
ADR 0013 took on measurement, and none of those decisions is in question.

**Documentation alone would not close it**, which is why item 2 is here. A convention that is
written down but unenforced is still violable, and the violation is still silent — the gate would
keep reporting agreement. Equally, enforcement alone would leave an author failing a check whose
rule is written nowhere. Each half is unsatisfying without the other, which is the argument for one
item rather than two.

**The present corpus is not affected.** All twenty-five standards follow the convention, twenty-six
of twenty-six entries agree, and no live defect exists. This is a guard's reach, not a corpus
repair — nothing here revisits [[FE-34]]'s six edits or reopens [[EP-15]].

**What this does not extend to.** Prohibitions stay outside level checking, for the reason ADR 0013
records: their sections quote a must-never bullet rather than stating a modal, and no level
convention exists for them. Establishing one is separate work and is still not filed.

**`NOT_STARTED` means not scheduled.** Everything the work needs is recorded, and nothing blocks
starting. It carries no release consequence: documenting a convention and adding a finding reason
changes no rule's level, adds no rule, and touches nothing on the frozen surface.
