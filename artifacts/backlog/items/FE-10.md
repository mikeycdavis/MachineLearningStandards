## What

`INSTRUCTIONS.md` plus six templates, including MODEL-CARD.md and DATASET-CARD.md — the
shipped compliant examples the model-card and data-card detectors look for.

## Why

Two detectors assert the presence of a document shape. Shipping the shape means a project
can satisfy them by doing the work rather than by guessing what the regex wants.

## Notes

`test/instructions.test.mjs` holds the guide to the code: every referenced path, script
and subcommand must exist, and the templates must validate against the real schema.
